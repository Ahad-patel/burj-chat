About Us

Years experience working
Since 1901

It was in this year that the Latif family first started business in Mumbai by becoming a contractor for Burmah Shell (now BPCL) for transporting oil and petrol. From then onward there was no looking back for the group

In 1962 the business expanded into the Timber and Interior industry sector.

In 1981 the business grew to incorporate an export-focused entity. Our experience and background give us a competitive advantage both in terms of procurement and quality control.

Operating throughout Mumbai, we are a company which is built on strong foundations of integrity and reliability.

At Burj Constructions, we love what we do, and we are extremely proud of the extensive range of projects that we have successfully delivered.

We provide a total solutions service covering project financing, property development, pre-construction management and design, alongside a first-class construction delivery capability.

Starting in 2009, our company has successfully redeveloped and delivered 500+residential and commercial units in south mumbai. We believe that “simplicity is the ultimate form of sophistication.” Our group plans to deliver approximately 500 more units in the following 3 years.

We have Developed area spanning upto 4 lakh sq.ft

We create flexible and functional facilities that meet the needs of our clients and end-users, now and in the future.
